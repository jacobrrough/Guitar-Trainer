Guitar samples (guitar-steel.mp3)

The recorded guitar in this app comes from the University of Iowa Electronic Music
Studios "Musical Instrument Samples" (steel-string acoustic guitar),
http://theremin.music.uiowa.edu/, as edited and published by Nicholaus P. Brosowsky
in tonejs-instruments, https://github.com/nbrosowsky/tonejs-instruments
(commit 622c2f1c32c8cfce4158ddc3eb26e518ddef37e5).

Licence of the samples: Creative Commons Attribution 3.0,
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/

Changes made for this app: each note trimmed to start at its attack and to four
seconds at most, faded out, packed into one file and encoded as MP3
(scripts/build-guitar-samples.mjs). The note A2 is the A#2 recording played a
semitone lower.
